Journal article Online

Africville and the Dynamics of State Power in Postwar Canada

2010-06-06

Uses the relocation of Africville's residents during Halifax's mid-1960s urban renewal program to examine how postwar state power actually worked. Argues that the case reveals more than racism alone, including the ideas of the good life built into the welfare state.

  • Tina Loo
  • Africville
  • Halifax

Register Online

No. 2 Construction Battalion: Nominal Roll

28 March 1917

A digitized nominal roll of the officers, non-commissioned officers and men of the No. 2 Construction Battalion, a First World War unit headquartered first in Pictou and later in Truro, with a companion database searchable by surname and community. Regimental numbers from the roll can be used to look up soldiers' attestation papers at Library and Archives Canada.

  • Truro
  • Pictou
  • Halifax

Robert Lawson and 11 Others (Front)

1785

Image and transcription of a 1785 land petition, labelled "Blacks of Maugerville", from Robert Lawson and eleven other Black men in Maugerville, New Brunswick, addressed to Thomas Carleton. It appears in the Fredericton Region Museum's online exhibit on early Black settlers of York-Sunbury counties; the original is credited to the Provincial Archives of New Brunswick.

Archival document Online & in person

Opinion juridique de Nicolas Guillet de Chaumont, conseiller et juge, deuxième interrogatoire sur la sellette, 4 juin 1734.

4 juin 1734

Transcription of a judge's legal opinion from the 1734 Montreal criminal trial of Marie-Josèphe-Angélique, an enslaved Black woman accused of arson, given at her second interrogation on the "sellette". The page cites the original file at the "Archives nationales du Québec, Centre de Montréal" (now part of BAnQ; that link is our identification).

  • Marie-Josèphe-Angélique

  • Thérèse de Couagne
